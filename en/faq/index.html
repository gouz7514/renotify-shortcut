Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 27 or later. The Notification automation that runs a shortcut when a notification arrives was introduced in iOS 27.

Where are my notifications stored?

Only on your iPhone. Notification content is never sent to a server, and there are no accounts, ads, or tracking.

What happens to my history when I switch phones?
  • If you move to your new iPhone with an iCloud backup or device-to-device transfer, your history comes with it.
  • If you reinstall the app, your history is deleted.
