Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 27 or later. The Notification automation that runs a shortcut when a notification arrives was introduced in iOS 27.

Where are my notifications stored?

Only on your iPhone. Notification content is never sent to a server, and there are no accounts, ads, or tracking.

To show app icons, Renotify looks up the app's identifier (for example, com.iwilab.KakaoTalk) on the App Store to download its icon. Notification content is not sent.

What happens when I switch phones?
  • If you move to your new iPhone with an iCloud backup or device-to-device transfer, your history comes with it.
  • If you just reinstall the app without a backup, your history starts empty.
  • On your new iPhone, check that automation is turned on for the Save Notifications to Renotify shortcut. If the shortcut is missing, add it again from Settings in Renotify.
